Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do I need prior Frisbee experience to join?

No. Beginners are welcome, and training will cover the basic skills and rules.

What should I bring to training?

Bring comfortable sportswear, suitable sports shoes, and a water bottle. Frisbees will be provided by the club.

When and where are training sessions held?

Training schedules and locations are announced on the Calendar & Events page.

How can I register for the club?

Complete the registration form on our website or contact the club for further information.

Can I participate in tournaments?

Yes. Members may have the opportunity to represent APU in tournaments depending on team selection and readiness.

Is the club suitable for beginners?

Yes. The club provides a friendly environment where beginners can learn, practice, and improve alongside experienced players.

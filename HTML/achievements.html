NUBR2026

Our competitive squad, the APU Pixels, delivered a phenomenal performance at the NUBR 2026 tournament organized by the UNM Ultimate Frisbee Club. Through relentless layouts, strategic play, and outstanding team synergy, the team fought hard against fierce university rivals and proudly claimed the Pool B Championship title! A historical milestone that showcases the growth and dedication of our club on the national stage

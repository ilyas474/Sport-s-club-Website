Ultimate Frisbee 10 Core Rules

help u to learn about frisbee rules.

Ultimate Frisbee places great responsibility on every player to be fair. There are no referees! Players self-referee all fouls, lines, and violations based on honesty and sportsmanship.

A standard field is rectangular with an end zone at each end. The main playing field is 64 meters long, and each end zone is 18 meters deep, making it 100 meters total.

Each point begins with both teams lining up on their respective end zone front lines. The defense throws (pulls) the disc to the offense to start the game.

A team scores a point when a player catches a legal pass inside the opponent’s end zone. The team with the most points at the end wins.

The disc may be advanced in any direction by completing a pass to a teammate. Players cannot run while holding the disc; they must stop and established a pivot foot.

The player holding the disc (the thrower) has 10 seconds to pass it. The defender guarding them (the marker) must loudly count "Stalling 1, 2, 3..." up to 10.

When a pass is not completed (e.g., hits the ground, goes out of bounds, gets intercepted or knocked down by a defender), the defense immediately takes possession and becomes the offense.

Ultimate Frisbee is a non-contact sport. No pushing, blocking, or physical tackling is allowed. Contact results in a foul violation.

When a player initiates contact on another player, a foul occurs. If the fouled player calls "Foul", play stops. If the offender agrees, possession is adjusted. If they disagree, the disc goes back to the previous thrower.

Players not in the game may substitute only after a point is scored, or if a player gets injured during a point. You cannot swap players during active play.

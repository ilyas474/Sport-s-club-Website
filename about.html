  WHO WE ARE  

Introduction

Established in 2023, the APU Frisbee Club is a student-led club at Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) that promotes the sport of Ultimate Frisbee.
The club welcomes players of all skill levels and provides regular training, friendly games, and opportunities to participate in tournaments.
It aims to encourage teamwork, sportsmanship, fitness, and friendship in a fun and inclusive environment.

Vision, Mission and Objectives

Vision

To be a leading university ultimate frisbee club that promotes excellence in sportsmanship, teamwork, and personal development.

Mission

To provide students with opportunities to learn, play, and compete in Ultimate Frisbee while building friendships and leadership skills.

Objectives

  • Promote Ultimate Frisbee.
  • Encourage teamwork.
  • Organize training sessions.
  • Participate in tournaments.


Our Core Values

We are guided by the values of teamwork, respect, commitment, sportsmanship, and inclusivity.
We believe in supporting one another, playing with integrity, continuously improving our skills,
and creating a welcoming environment where every member can learn, grow, and enjoy the spirit of Ultimate Frisbee.



Why Join Us?

Be part of a fun and welcoming community where you can stay fit,
improve your frisbee skills, build teamwork, and make lasting friendships.
No experience is required—just bring your enthusiasm!

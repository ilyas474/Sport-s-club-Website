Ultimate Frisbee Tournament Brings Teams Together

🥏 National Ultimate Frisbee Tournament Draws Teams from Across

Kuala Lumpur, 6 September 2026 — Ultimate Frisbee players from across Malaysia gathered this weekend for an exciting tournament featuring competitive matches, teamwork, and outstanding displays of athleticism. Teams competed throughout the day in a series of intense matches, with players demonstrating speed, accurate throwing, and strong defensive strategies. Spectators were also treated to several close games that kept the competition exciting until the final points. The tournament highlighted the growing popularity of Ultimate Frisbee among students and young athletes in Malaysia. Organisers expressed their hope that more events like this will encourage new players to join the sport and promote the values of teamwork, respect, and Spirit of the Game. The event concluded with an award ceremony recognising the winning teams and outstanding players.
